About Me

Hi, I'm Rupesh Ravu — a self-taught graphic designer, creative thinker, and Computer Science and Information Technology student at KL University.

My journey into design started long before I had professional experience or access to advanced design tools. I began experimenting with designs during my lockdown days, exploring creativity and learning everything on my own.

Canva was my first step into the world of design. Later, when I entered B.Tech, I started learning Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, and InDesign. One of my proudest moments was learning Photoshop in just one week during my second year, first semester. I believe curiosity, consistency, and practice can take you further than you imagine.

What defines me

I believe good design is not just about making something look attractive. It's about communicating an idea, creating an experience, and making people feel something.

The Journey Behind My Designs

My journey at KL University took an unexpected turn just two days before SAMYAK Fest during my first-year, first-semester days. I joined the SAMYAK team as a Designing Coordinator.

During my second semester, I designed seven website designs. Those designs caught the attention of the team, and I got an opportunity to work with SURABHI, our university's fest held in March. That opportunity opened another door.

I went on to create the main designs for SAMYAK 2025. My work and contribution earned me a higher position in the SAMYAK designing team during my second year. That was the point where my journey as a designer truly started growing.

From being a coordinator to taking responsibility for major creative designs, every opportunity taught me something new. Each fest, each project, and every challenge helped shape the designer I am today.

I didn't start with everything figured out. I learned, experimented, made mistakes, and improved with every design. And I'm still learning, still creating, and still exploring what's possible.
